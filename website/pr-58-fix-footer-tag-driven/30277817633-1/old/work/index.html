DollhouseMCP#

Website: dollhousemcp.com | GitHub: DollhouseMCP/mcp-server | License: AGPL-3.0

A Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for dynamic AI persona management. Hot-swap Personas, Skills, Templates, Agents, and Memory Elements without restarting. Works with Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other MCP-compatible systems.

MCP-AQL#

Website: mcpaql.com | Spec: MCPAQL/spec | Examples: MCPAQL/examples | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21398630

A query layer over MCP tools. Every substrate that gets widely adopted grows one eventually: SQL did it for storage, GraphQL for REST APIs, and tool sprawl is forcing the same thing here. MCP-AQL is a way to address, query, and compose across tools instead of calling them one at a time. It is in daily use rather than theoretical, and most of what I do with DollhouseMCP now runs through it.

AILIS#

Website: ailis.dollhouseresearch.com | GitHub: DollhouseMCP/AILIS | DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21398506

An AI Layer Interface Specification: a proposed sixteen-layer reference model for the AI stack, in the spirit of what OSI did for networking. The bet is that a shared vocabulary for where capabilities sit makes it easier to see what is missing. Early draft, open for argument.

Elemental Surveys#

Website: elementalsurveys.com | Work samples: elementalsurveys.com/work

Synthetic-first market research. Fast synthetic survey reads that pressure-test concepts, messaging, and audience assumptions before the expensive commitment is made, for teams that need signal while the idea is still movable.

Director of Product - Web | Jan 2019 - Apr 2019 | San Francisco, CA

Managed the Product team for website (ranging from 4 to 7 engineers and designers) for The Alliance of American Football (AAF.com) building out a full featured sports league website with stats, news, live telemetry, streaming video, video on demand, an online store, site security, internal web tools, and more.
